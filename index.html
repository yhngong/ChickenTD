HOW TO PLAY CHICKENTD

  • 34 PIXEL CHICKENS & GEM EGG GACHA: Start with the 14 classic troops up to Cyber-Cluck 9000. Earn 20 Mythic & Battle-Cats Units above Cyber-Cluck 9000 (Macho Cat-Hen, Eraser Shield-Hen, Surge Priestess, Awakened Baha-Cluck, Gaia Mother-Hen, Golden Cluck-God & more) by cracking Gem Eggs in the Main Menu Shop!
  • SAVED EGGS CURRENCY (1 PER 2,500 BUGS): Every 2,500 Bugs popped earns 1 Egg (leftover progress carries over and saves after every round!). Spend Eggs in the Gem Egg Shop (`1x`, `4x`, or `8x` multi-hatch!).
  • 4-TIER MUTATIONS & +LEVELS: Hatching a Chicken you already own awakens a Mutation (Common 55%, Rare 28%, Epic 13%, Legendary 4%) plus permanent Battle Cats +Levels!
  • BATTLE CATS CLUCK CANNON & TRAITS: Charge your Cluck Cannon (`[TAB]`) during waves to blast & knock back every enemy on the map, and deploy units with Knockback & 3x Savage Blow Crits!
  • ZERO BUG GOLD + 12 CPS CLICKER: Enemies drop 0 Corn! Click the Henhouse (max 12 CPS) and hire Farmhand Chicks during active waves to fund your defense!
  • TRUE CAMO IMMUNITY: Camo enemies take 0 damage without Camo Sight (or Epic/Legendary Mutations / Watchtower Roost Lv3).
