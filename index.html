HOW TO PLAY CHICKENTD

  • 39 PIXEL CHICKENS & THE COMPLETE CLUCK: Start with 16 Chickens up to Cyber-Cluck 9000, unlock 22 more from Gem Eggs in the Shop, and collect all 38 to forge The Complete Cluck (Terraria Zenith blade — swappable between Mastered & Aether)!
  • GEM EGGS & AUTO-SAVE (1 PER 1,000 KILLS): Every 1,000 Enemies defeated gives you 1 Gem Egg directly! Open them `1x`, `4x`, or `8x` at a time — your whole game saves automatically!
  • 4-TIER MUTATIONS & +LEVELS: Hatching a Chicken you already own awakens a Mutation (Common 55%, Rare 28%, Epic 13%, Legendary 4%) plus permanent Battle Cats +Levels!
  • 5 CLUCK CANNONS (EQUIP IN SHOP BEFORE BATTLE!): Unlock & equip your Cluck Cannon (Shockwave, Time-Stop, Damage Boost, Orbital Nova, or Cornstorm) in the Shop with Gem Eggs! Your chosen cannon is locked in for the entire run (`[TAB]` to fire).
  • ZERO BUG GOLD + 12 CPS CLICKER: Enemies drop 0 Corn! Click the Henhouse (max 12 CPS) and hire Farmhand Chicks during active waves to fund your defense!
  • TRUE CAMO IMMUNITY: Camo enemies take 0 damage without Camo Sight (or Epic/Legendary Mutations / Watchtower Roost Lv3).
  • 5 DIFFICULTIES (INCLUDING TRUE FOX-HUNT!) & IDLE MODE: Choose from Easy up to Nightmare (`2.4x` swarms) or True Fox-Hunt (`+40%` costs & Fox Bosses ambushing way earlier starting Wave 3!), or click GO IDLE (`~1 Gem Egg per 10 mins`)!
