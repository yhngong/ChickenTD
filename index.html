🐔 How to Play ChickenTD

  • Protect the Golden Nest: Bugs (Bloons-style multi-layer pests!) and Giant Foxes (MOAB-class bosses that burst into smaller bugs!) march down the farm path toward your Nest Eggs.
  • Bugs & Foxes Drop $0: Popping enemies gives no money! 100% of your Corn comes from clicking the Henhouse Clicker.
  • Active-Wave Clicking Only (12 CPS Cap): You can only click to earn Corn while a wave is active (locked between waves!), and clicking is strictly capped at 12 CPS—any faster clicks do nothing.
  • Strategic Tradeoff: Every kernel forces a choice—invest in Clicker Upgrades for bigger future harvests, or deploy/upgrade Chicken Troops & TD Roost Lab before the Foxes overrun your Nest!
