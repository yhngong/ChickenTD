# ChickenTD

A 16-bit pixel-art mashup of an **active Clicker game**, **Bloons TD-inspired layered defense & support towers**, and **Battle Cats gacha, mutations, & cannon combat**! Choose across **5 Pixel-Art Biome Maps** and **4 Difficulties**, deploy and upgrade **38 specialized pixel-art Chicken Troops** (including 4 Bloons-style **Support Aura** Chickens), fire your rechargeable **Cluck Cannon**, and earn **Gem Eggs** directly (`1 Gem Egg per 1,000 Enemies killed`) to crack open new units and **4-Tier Mutations**.

## Core Mechanics

- **Main Menu — 5 Maps, 4 Swarm Difficulties, Idle Mode & Gem Egg Hatchery**:
  - **Maps**: *Sunny Clover Meadow* (`Beginner`), *Frostbite Pine Fjord* (`Intermediate`), *Scorched Roost Caldera* (`Advanced`), *Whispering Honey-Bog* (`Expert`), and *Cosmic Egg Sanctum* (`Master`).
  - **Swarm Difficulties (Faster Gem Eggs!)**: Higher difficulties keep Enemy HP at `1.0x` instead of making enemies sponge hits, and instead spawn **larger swarms** — *Chickadee* (`0.75x` Enemies), *Barnyard* (`1.0x` Enemies), *Fox-Hunt* (`1.65x` Enemies), and *Henpocalypse* (`2.4x` Enemies) — so skilled players earn Gem Eggs up to **2.4x faster**!
  - **Brawler Bantam Idle Mode (`GO IDLE`)**: Click **GO IDLE** from the Main Menu, Gem Egg Hatchery, or Top HUD to watch **Brawler Bantam** patrol the pasture and strike down strolling enemies (`100` kills/min = **1 Gem Egg every 10 minutes**), safely pausing your active battle and tracking wall-clock time even in background tabs!
- **Battle Cats Progression — Gem Eggs, Full Auto-Save & Mutations**:
  - **Direct Gem Egg Drops**: Earn **1 Gem Egg per 1,000 Enemies killed** (kill progress carries over across rounds and saves automatically!).
  - **Full Game Auto-Save**: Automatically saves your active run (wave, Corn, Nest HP, placed Chickens, tiers, upgrades, and enemies) alongside your Gem Eggs, unlocked units, and Mutations in `localStorage`.
  - **Starter vs. Gem Egg Roster**: You start with the **16 classic Chickens up to Cyber-Cluck 9000** (including **Roost Beacon** & **Espresso Brew-Hen** support towers). All **22 units after Cyber-Cluck 9000** are unlocked by opening your **Gem Eggs** in the Main Menu!
  - **1x, 4x & 8x Multi-Hatch Cutscene**: Crack open **1 Gem Egg**, **4 Gem Eggs at a time**, or **8 Gem Eggs at a time** in an animated crystalline egg-shattering cutscene!
  - **4-Tier Duplicate Mutations & `+Levels`**: Pulling a unit you already own awakens a **Mutation** (**Common `55%`**, **Rare `28%`**, **Epic `13%`**, **Legendary `4%`**) plus permanent Battle Cats **`+Levels`**!
- **Battle Cats Combat Features & Defeat Endscreen**:
  - **Cluck Cannon (`[TAB]`)**: Charges during active waves and unleashes a full-lane shockwave beam that damages, slows, and knocks back every Bug and Fox Boss on the map!
  - **Knockback & 3x Savage Blows**: Deploy specialized Battle-Cats-inspired units that knock enemies backward along the path and land explosive **3x Savage Crit** strikes!
  - **Battle Cats Defeat Report**: Detailed endscreen when you lose showing Boss HP Remaining % / Stage Wave Progress %, Bugs Popped, MVP Chicken Unit, and Gem Egg rewards!
- **Bloons-Style Nearby Support Aura Towers**:
  - Deploy **Roost Beacon** (`Village`), **Espresso Brew-Hen** (`Alchemist`), **Overclock Engie-Hen** (`Engineer`), and **Shrine Idol-Hen** (`Temple Idol`) to buff nearby Chickens' range, attack speed, damage, pierce, Camo Sight, Armor-Piercing, Savage Crit, and multishot!
- **100% Handcrafted Pixel-Art Graphics**:
  - Custom nearest-neighbor `460x300` pixel-art biome stages, live baked pixel-art map thumbnails, multi-stage Gem Egg sprites, and bespoke pixel-art sprites for all **38 Chicken classes**, **10 Bug types**, **9 Giant Fox Bosses**, and UI icons (zero emoji sprites).
- **Zero Enemy Gold — 100% Henhouse Economy (`12 CPS` Cap)**:
  - Popping Bugs and Foxes awards **0 Corn**! Harvest Corn during active waves at the **Henhouse Clicker** (capped at `12 CPS`) alongside passive **Farmhand Chicks** and **9 Clicker Upgrades**.

## Controls

| Input | Action |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Pointer Click on Henhouse** | Harvest Corn during active waves (strictly capped at `12 CPS`) |
| `1`–`9`, `0`, `-`, `=`, `Q`–`P`, `A`–`L`, `Z`–`M` | Select any unlocked Chicken Troop to deploy |
| `Tab` / **Cluck Cannon Button** | Fire Battle Cats Cluck Cannon when 100% charged |
| **Click Placed Chicken** | Open Inspector to Upgrade (`Tier 1–4`), change Targeting, or Sell |
| `Space` | Start Next Wave (or Skip Gem Egg Cutscene) |
| `Esc` / Right-Click | Cancel placement or close inspector / shop |
